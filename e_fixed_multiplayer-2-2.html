Fighter Manual
OVERVIEW [MANUAL_01]

Welcome to THE LAND OF DESOLATION — a 2-round fighting game where two elemental warriors clash for dominance. Win 2 rounds to claim the match. Each round ends when time expires or a fighter's HP hits zero. Best of 3.

RESOURCE BARS [MANUAL_03]

❤ Vitality (HP): Your life bar. Depleted to zero means the round is lost. Does not recover between rounds.

⚡ Stamina: Consumed by dashes and special moves. Regenerates passively. Watch for the glow — full stamina unlocks full-power dashes.

🛡 Defense: Blocking absorbs damage but drains this bar. If it hits zero your defense breaks and you stagger — no blocking until it recovers.

◎ Frenzy / Fire Meter: Fills by dealing and taking damage. When full, activating your ultimate unleashes your character's most powerful state.

MOVEMENT & COMBAT [MANUAL_04]

• Double Jump: Press Jump a second time while airborne to reach higher positions or extend air time. Costs 20 Stamina for the second jump. Resets on landing.

• Dash: A burst of speed in your current facing direction. Full Stamina (100) gives a long dash; half Stamina (50) gives a shorter one. 30-frame cooldown after use.

• Block: Hold Block to absorb attacks at the cost of Defense. Blocked hits deal reduced knockback and build your Frenzy meter faster than they build the attacker's.

• Parry: Press Block in the exact moment you're about to be hit (8-frame window) to Parry. The attacker gets stunned, knocked back, and their combo is reset. High risk, massive reward.

• Auto-Tracking: Ground attacks auto-correct toward the opponent within a short range so light chains land reliably without manual micro-positioning.

COMBO SYSTEM [MANUAL_05]

Landing hits in quick succession extends your combo counter. Combos have automatic damage scaling — each additional hit beyond the first deals less damage (scaling caps at 50% by hit 6+), preventing infinite loops.

• Air Launches: The 3rd hit and beyond launches the opponent airborne. Follow up with additional hits to keep them in the air.

• Air Combos (Double Jump): Use your double jump (Jumps Left = 0) and then press Light or Heavy to fire projectile waves mid-air. Chain Light → Heavy for the HL Combo, or use both simultaneously for a rainbow burst.

• Combo Breaker: While taking a combo hit, press Heavy with 40+ Stamina to burst out and escape. Costs Defense durability. Defense-broken fighters cannot use Combo Breaker.

• Dash Escape: Dashing during the opponent's active combo has a 30% chance to break their combo count automatically.

COMMAND LIST — CRYOSAMA [MANUAL_06A]

THE CRYO WARRIOR — Control ice, freeze opponents, create openings

Hailstorm Block → Forward + Light Fires 3 kunai-spread projectiles. Excellent zoning and meter building. In Frenzy the clone fires simultaneously.
Boreas Pillar Block → Block + Heavy Erupts a crystal spike under the opponent — big damage and full launch. Holds cast pose until the pillar fades. Clone mirrors it in Frenzy.
Frosty Beam Back → Forward + Dash Fires a screen-wide cryo beam. High damage, stuns on hit. Strong punish for whiffed specials across the stage.
Frost Flash Forward → Forward + Heavy A blazing dash through the opponent. Leaves after-images and carries significant knockback.
❄ Winter's Wrath Light + Heavy (Full Meter) ULTIMATE. Activates Frenzy — boosts damage 25%, spawns an independent Clone that stalks behind the enemy. Drains opponent HP continuously for 10 seconds.
COMMAND LIST — PYRELIA [MANUAL_06B]

THE FLAME SOVEREIGN — Aggressive pressure, fire coverage, devastating ultimates

Inferno Kick Forward → Forward + Light A lunging flaming kick that closes distance fast. Good combo starter and punish tool. Carries momentum — use to cross screen quickly.
Fire Breath Block → Block + Light Pyrelia exhales a sustained cone of fire. Multi-hit, hits repeatedly while active. Strong up-close pressure and wall carry. Forces the opponent to move or block.
Meteor Strike Forward → Back + Heavy Calls down a pixel-art meteor directly on the opponent's position. A warning reticle appears on the ground first — bait the enemy to stay still. High damage on impact, massive explosion burst.
Fire Storm Block → Forward + Heavy Summons a single rising column of fire that sweeps once across the entire stage in the direction you're facing, then disappears. One hit only per cast — damage falls off with distance from the eruption point: 54 damage point-blank, tapering down to 18 by the far edge of the sweep.
🌋 Eruption Overlord Light + Heavy (Full Meter) ULTIMATE. Spawns a towering pixel-art volcano at center stage. For 7 seconds it launches pixel fireballs and rock debris at the opponent. Boosts Pyrelia's damage 25%. Fireballs home in on enemy position.
ADVANCED MECHANICS [MANUAL_07]

Defense Breaking: Sustained block pressure depletes the blue Defense bar. A depleted defender staggers and cannot block for 20 seconds. The attacker should immediately press the advantage with a special.

Clone (Cryosama): During Winter's Wrath the clone hugs the enemy's back and acts on its own — throwing its own light attacks roughly once a second at half damage. Whenever you cast a special (Hailstorm, Boreas Pillar, Frosty Beam, Frost Flash), the clone copy-casts the same special 0.1s later from its own position, also at half damage.

Volcano Debris (Pyrelia): Regular fireballs deal 6 damage; the meteor (Meteor Strike) deals 38 with a huge splash radius. During Eruption Overlord the debris fires every 20 frames — roughly 3 projectiles per second, for 7 seconds total.

AI Difficulty: Easy bots react slowly and never use specials. Medium bots block and punish reliably. Hard bots parry, anti-air, use all specials, and wall-pressure with near-perfect timing.

HOW TO EXECUTE SPECIAL MOVES ON TOUCH

Special moves use motion sequences — combine directional taps with attack buttons just like keyboard.

Forward → Forward + L Tap ▶ twice quickly, then tap ⚡L — fires Inferno Kick / Frost Flash.
Block → Forward + L Hold 🛡, tap ▶, then tap ⚡L — fires Hailstorm / Fire Breath.
Block → Block + H Tap 🛡 twice, then tap 🔥H — fires Boreas Pillar.
Back → Forward + DASH Tap ◀, then ▶ + DASH simultaneously — fires Frosty Beam.
⚡L + 🔥H (Full Meter) Tap both attack buttons at the same time to unleash your Ultimate.
📱 MOBILE TIPS

📐 Landscape is best: Rotate your device sideways for the full-width arena view. The HUD auto-scales in landscape so nothing is cropped.

👆 Multi-touch works: Both thumbs can register taps simultaneously — hold a direction while tapping attack buttons freely.

⚡ Parry on mobile: Tap 🛡 at the very last moment before a hit lands. The 8-frame parry window is tight — practice the rhythm against Easy AI first.

🔄 Combo Breaker: During hitstun, quickly tap 🔥H while your opponent is chaining — same timing as keyboard, but use a sharp single tap, not a press-and-hold.

💨 Dash shortcut: Tap DASH once for a quick burst. Make sure your Stamina bar is at least half full (yellow glow) first — a low-stamina dash wastes your turn.

🎮 Single-player tip: In Vs A.I. mode only one pad (left side) is needed. The AI controls Player 2 automatically — ignore the right pad entirely.

⏸ Pause: Tap the ⏸ PAUSE button in the centre-top timer area. From the pause menu you can resume or return to the main menu without losing your round progress.

TIPS & TRICKS [MANUAL_08]

🔥 Fire Storm into Ultimate: Fire Storm's sweep now hits at most once, but the burn window still leaves an opening — land it, then use a combo to fill your meter and chain into Eruption Overlord.

❄ Pillar as anti-air: Boreas Pillar spawns at the opponent's feet no matter where they are. Use it to punish opponents who jump predictably — the launch velocity sends them straight up for a juggle follow-up.

🎯 Meteor read: Meteor Strike locks onto the opponent's position at cast time. If they're dashing toward you, lead the cast slightly behind them. The reticle on the ground tells them where it's coming — use it as a mind game.

💨 Stamina management: Never dash if you're below 50 Stamina — the short dash barely moves you and leaves you open. Save for full-stamina dashes or special move activations.

🛡 Parry bait: Tick a light attack from max range, then walk in and throw a heavy as the opponent presses Block expecting a parry window. Their defense drops, yours doesn't.

🌀 Combo Breaker timing: The break fires on Heavy press during hitstun — not on release. Tap it early in the stun window before your defense breaks further. Each break costs Defense durability.

🌋 Fire Breath pressure: Fire Breath's cone hits multiple times and each hit builds Fire Meter. Hold it at point-blank range against a blocking opponent to shred their Defense bar before they can counter.

🪞 Clone positioning: The clone always stalks the enemy's back side. It fights independently now — its own jabs and copy-cast specials land on their own timers, so a cornered opponent is getting pressured from two angles instead of eating a doubled hit on everything you throw.
